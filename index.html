Frequently asked questions

How much does social media video production cost in the Central Valley?

Most local businesses invest between $500 and $2,500 per month depending on how many videos they need. Our monthly packages start at $750/month and include filming, editing, captions, and posting strategy — with a 3-month minimum and no long-term contracts.

What types of businesses benefit most from Reels and TikToks?

Restaurants, gyms, salons, med spas, real estate agents, auto shops, and retail stores see the fastest results, because customers choose these businesses visually. If people can see your work, video will sell it.

Do you film on location in Fresno and surrounding cities?

Yes — we film at your business anywhere in the 559, from Fresno and Clovis to Visalia, Selma, Hanford, and Madera. A typical shoot takes 1–2 hours and produces a full month of content.

How long until I see results?

Most businesses see increased reach and profile visits within 30 days of posting consistently. Meaningful growth in followers, inquiries, and foot traffic usually shows in 60–90 days at 3+ videos per week.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Plenty of high-performing videos feature your product, your space, your team, or your process. If you do want to be on camera, we'll coach you so it feels easy and natural.
